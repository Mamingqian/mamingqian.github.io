A DNA sequence and its reverse complement often carry the same biological meaning, yet DNA language models frequently make inconsistent predictions on the two strands. Reverse-Complement Consistency Regularization (RCCR) is a simple, model-agnostic fine-tuning objective that penalizes the divergence between a model's prediction on a sequence and the aligned prediction on its reverse complement. It is evaluated on Nucleotide Transformer, HyenaDNA, and DNABERT-2 across sequence classification, scalar regression, and profile prediction tasks.
